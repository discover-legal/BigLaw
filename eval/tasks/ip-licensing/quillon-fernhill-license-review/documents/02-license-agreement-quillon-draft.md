1.1 "Field" means autonomous agricultural harvesting robots.

1.2 "Licensed Patents" means the patents and patent applications listed in Schedule A, and all continuations, divisionals and reissues of them.

1.3 "Licensed Software" means the GraspCore control software in object-code form, as listed with its third-party components in Schedule B.

1.4 "Licensed Product" means any robot or robotic system made, used or sold by or for Licensee that incorporates a GraspCore gripper or the Licensed Software.

2.1 Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable license under the Licensed Patents and the Licensed Software to make, use, sell and import Licensed Products in the Field throughout the world.

## 4. ROYALTIES

4.1 Licensee shall pay Licensor a running royalty of five and one-half percent (5.5%) of Gross Sales of Licensed Products.
