# LOGISTICS SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Logistics Services Agreement is dated June 1, 2024 between **Meridian Freight Services, Inc.**, an Ohio corporation ("Carrier"), and **Brightwater Sensor Systems, Inc.** ("Shipper").

## 1. SERVICES

1.1 Carrier shall provide inbound freight, warehousing and outbound parcel and less-than-truckload delivery services for Shipper's products within the continental United States, as described in the Rate Schedule.

## 2. CHARGES

2.1 Shipper shall pay the charges in the Rate Schedule within thirty (30) days after invoice. Fuel surcharges are adjusted monthly by reference to the published national diesel index.

2.2 Annual charges under this Agreement were approximately $410,000 in fiscal year 2025.

## 6. TERM

6.1 The term of this Agreement is two (2) years from the date hereof and continues month to month thereafter until terminated by either party on sixty (60) days' notice.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT

9.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's consent, except to an Affiliate. For the avoidance of doubt, a change of control of either party, including any merger or sale of stock, shall not constitute an assignment of this Agreement and shall not require the consent of the other party.

## 11. LIABILITY

11.1 Carrier's liability for loss of or damage to goods is limited to $5.00 per pound unless Shipper declares a higher value and pays the applicable excess-value charge.
