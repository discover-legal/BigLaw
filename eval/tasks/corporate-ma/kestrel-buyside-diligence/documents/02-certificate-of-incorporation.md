# AMENDED AND RESTATED CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION OF BRIGHTWATER SENSOR SYSTEMS, INC.

*(Filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware on March 3, 2023)*

**ARTICLE I — NAME.** The name of the corporation is Brightwater Sensor Systems, Inc. (the "Corporation").

**ARTICLE II — REGISTERED OFFICE.** The address of the Corporation's registered office in the State of Delaware is 251 Little Falls Drive, Wilmington, New Castle County, Delaware 19808.

**ARTICLE III — PURPOSE.** The purpose of the Corporation is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which corporations may be organized under the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware.

**ARTICLE IV — AUTHORIZED STOCK.** The total number of shares of all classes of stock which the Corporation shall have authority to issue is 11,500,000, consisting of 10,000,000 shares of Common Stock, $0.0001 par value per share, and 1,500,000 shares of Preferred Stock, $0.0001 par value per share, all of which are designated "Series A Preferred Stock."

**ARTICLE V — RIGHTS OF SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK.**

**Section 1. Dividends.** Holders of Series A Preferred Stock shall be entitled to receive non-cumulative dividends, when and if declared by the Board of Directors, at the rate of 6% of the Original Issue Price per annum, prior and in preference to any dividend on the Common Stock.

**Section 2. Liquidation Preference.**

2.1 In the event of any liquidation, dissolution or winding up of the Corporation, or any Deemed Liquidation Event, the holders of Series A Preferred Stock shall be entitled to be paid out of the assets available for distribution, before any payment to the holders of Common Stock, an amount per share equal to one and one-half times (1.5x) the Series A Original Issue Price of $5.00 per share, plus any declared but unpaid dividends.

2.2 After payment in full of the amounts in Section 2.1, the remaining assets available for distribution shall be distributed ratably among the holders of Series A Preferred Stock and Common Stock on an as-converted basis, so that the Series A Preferred Stock participates fully with the Common Stock.

2.3 **Deemed Liquidation Events.** Each of the following shall be a "Deemed Liquidation Event": (a) a merger or consolidation in which the Corporation is a constituent party, unless the stockholders immediately prior to such transaction hold a majority of the voting power of the surviving entity; (b) the sale, in a single transaction or series of related transactions, of shares representing more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting power of the Corporation; and (c) the sale, lease or exclusive license of all or substantially all of the assets of the Corporation.

**Section 3. Conversion.** Each share of Series A Preferred Stock shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into one share of Common Stock, subject to adjustment.

**Section 4. Protective Provisions.** So long as at least 300,000 shares of Series A Preferred Stock are outstanding, the Corporation shall not, either directly or indirectly by amendment, merger, consolidation or otherwise, effect or validate any Deemed Liquidation Event without the written consent or affirmative vote of the holders of at least sixty percent (60%) of the then outstanding shares of Series A Preferred Stock, given in writing or by vote at a meeting, consenting or voting separately as a class.

**ARTICLE VI — RESTRICTIONS ON TRANSFER.**

6.1 **Right of First Refusal.** No stockholder shall sell, assign or otherwise transfer any shares of capital stock of the Corporation (other than to a Permitted Transferee) unless such stockholder has first offered such shares to the Corporation on the same terms, and the Corporation shall have thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice of the proposed transfer within which to elect to purchase all or any portion of such shares.

6.2 **Waiver.** The right of first refusal in Section 6.1 may be waived only by a resolution of the Board of Directors adopted with the approval of the director designated by the holders of Series A Preferred Stock.

6.3 Any transfer made in violation of this Article VI shall be null and void and shall not be recorded on the books of the Corporation.

**ARTICLE VII — DIRECTORS.** The number of directors shall be fixed from time to time by the Bylaws. The holders of Series A Preferred Stock, voting as a separate class, shall be entitled to elect one (1) director of the Corporation.

**ARTICLE VIII — LIMITATION OF DIRECTOR LIABILITY.** To the fullest extent permitted by the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware, a director of the Corporation shall not be personally liable to the Corporation or its stockholders for monetary damages for breach of fiduciary duty as a director.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Corporation has caused this Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation to be signed by its Chief Executive Officer on March 3, 2023.
