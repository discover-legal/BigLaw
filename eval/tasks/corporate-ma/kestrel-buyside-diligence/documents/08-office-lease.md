# OFFICE AND LIGHT INDUSTRIAL LEASE

**Landlord:** Corrigan Park Properties LLC
**Tenant:** Brightwater Sensor Systems, Inc.
**Premises:** 2250 Corrigan Park Drive, Building C, San Jose, California (approximately 38,400 rentable square feet)
**Lease Date:** April 1, 2022

## 1. TERM

1.1 The Term commences on April 1, 2022 and expires on June 30, 2027 (the "Expiration Date").

## 2. RENT

2.1 Base Rent for the period July 1, 2026 through the Expiration Date is $96,000 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each month.

## 3. OPTION TO EXTEND

3.1 Tenant shall have one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years at ninety-five percent (95%) of Fair Market Rent. Tenant must exercise the option by written notice delivered to Landlord no later than September 30, 2026. If Tenant fails to deliver such notice on or before such date, the option shall lapse and be of no further force or effect.

## 4. USE

4.1 The Premises may be used for general office, research and development, and light assembly of electronic sensor devices, and for no other purpose.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

15.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

15.2 Any transfer, in one or more transactions, of more than forty-nine percent (49%) of the voting stock of Tenant shall be deemed an assignment of this Lease requiring Landlord's prior written consent under Section 15.1.

## 22. SECURITY DEPOSIT

22.1 Tenant has deposited with Landlord a letter of credit in the amount of $288,000 as security for Tenant's obligations.
