# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Executive Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 10, 2023 between **Brightwater Sensor Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and **Daniel Okafor** ("Executive"), residing in San Jose, California.

## 1. POSITION AND DUTIES

1.1 Executive shall serve as Chief Executive Officer of the Company, reporting to the Board of Directors, and shall perform his duties principally from the Company's office in San Jose, California.

## 2. COMPENSATION

2.1 **Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $385,000, subject to annual review by the Board.

2.2 **Annual Bonus.** Executive shall be eligible for an annual target bonus of 50% of Base Salary based on achievement of performance goals set by the Board.

2.3 **Equity.** Executive has been granted an option to purchase 180,000 shares of Common Stock under the 2021 Equity Incentive Plan, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff.

## 5. CHANGE IN CONTROL

5.1 **Change in Control Payment.** Upon the consummation of a Change in Control, the Company shall pay Executive a lump-sum cash payment equal to two times (2.0x) Executive's then-current Base Salary (the "Change in Control Payment"), payable within ten (10) days after the Change in Control, whether or not Executive's employment is terminated.

5.2 **Equity Acceleration.** Upon the consummation of a Change in Control, one hundred percent (100%) of Executive's then-unvested equity awards shall immediately vest and become exercisable.

5.3 **No Cutback.** If any payment or benefit under this Agreement would constitute a "parachute payment" within the meaning of Section 280G of the Internal Revenue Code, Executive shall receive the full amount of such payments and the Company shall have no obligation to reduce them.

5.4 "Change in Control" means a sale of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting stock of the Company or a sale of all or substantially all of its assets.

## 7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 **Non-Competition.** During Executive's employment and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Executive shall not, anywhere in North America, engage in or provide services to any business that competes with the Company.

7.2 **Non-Solicitation.** For twenty-four (24) months following termination, Executive shall not solicit any employee or customer of the Company.

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
