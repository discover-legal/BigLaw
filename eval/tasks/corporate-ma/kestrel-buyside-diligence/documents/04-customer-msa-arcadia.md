# MASTER SUPPLY AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Agreement No. AUC-2022-0417**

This Master Supply and Services Agreement is made as of September 1, 2022 between **Arcadia Utilities Corp.**, a Pennsylvania corporation ("Customer"), and **Brightwater Sensor Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Vendor").

## 1. SCOPE

1.1 Vendor shall supply HydroSense network pressure monitors (the "Devices") and the HydroSense Cloud monitoring service (the "Services") to Customer under purchase orders and statements of work issued from time to time.

1.2 Customer has prepaid $1,150,000 for Services to be rendered during calendar years 2026 and 2027 (the "Prepaid Fees").

## 2. PRICING

2.1 **Most Favored Customer.** Vendor represents that the prices charged to Customer are, and shall remain during the Term, no less favorable than the prices charged by Vendor to any other utility customer purchasing comparable volumes. If Vendor offers more favorable pricing to any such customer, Vendor shall promptly extend that pricing to Customer retroactively to the date it was first offered.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 This Agreement has an initial term ending August 31, 2027, and renews for successive one-year periods unless either party gives ninety (90) days' notice of non-renewal.

9.2 Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice.

## 13. ASSIGNMENT; CHANGE OF CONTROL

13.1 Vendor shall not assign or transfer this Agreement, in whole or in part, including by merger, operation of law or Change of Control, without the prior written consent of Customer.

13.2 If Vendor undergoes a Change of Control without Customer's prior written consent, or if Vendor becomes controlled by a Competitor of Customer, Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice, and Vendor shall refund all Prepaid Fees attributable to Services not yet rendered as of the termination date.

13.3 "Competitor of Customer" means any person engaged in the business of supplying water or gas utility services, or any person that owns or operates utility metering or network monitoring assets in competition with Customer, and expressly includes Halberd Industrial Holdings, Inc. and its Affiliates.

13.4 "Change of Control" means any transaction or series of transactions by which any person acquires, directly or indirectly, more than forty percent (40%) of the voting equity of Vendor.

## 15. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

15.1 Except for a party's indemnification obligations, breach of Section 11 (Data Security) or gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
