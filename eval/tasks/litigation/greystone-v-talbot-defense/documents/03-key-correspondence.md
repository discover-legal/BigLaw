### Item 3 — Talbot notice of breach, June 3, 2025

**From:** Priya Venkataraman, Director of Quality Assurance, Talbot Fresh Foods
**To:** Harold Imbriano, President, Greystone Packaging Co.
**Date:** June 3, 2025
**Re:** Notice of material breach under Section 9.2 — laminate delamination

This is written notice under Section 9.2 of the Packaging Supply Agreement. Lots GP-25-1187 through GP-25-1203 delivered in May 2025 show laminate delamination at the seal in 11% of sampled pouches, against a 2% rejection threshold under Section 7.2. Delaminated pouches lose their barrier and cause spoilage. Please cure within thirty (30) days of this notice.

---

### Item 4 — Greystone reply, June 20, 2025

**From:** Harold Imbriano, President, Greystone Packaging Co.
**To:** Priya Venkataraman; cc Celeste Marchetti
**Date:** June 20, 2025

Priya — our engineers have traced the delamination to an adhesive application fault on Line 4 that began after a coater rebuild in April. We have adjusted the coat weight and expect conforming product from Line 4 by July 15. We apologize for the disruption. — Harold

---

### Item 5 — Talbot QA report, July 22, 2025

**From:** Priya Venkataraman, Director of Quality Assurance
**To:** Celeste Marchetti
**Date:** July 22, 2025
**Re:** Post-cure lots still failing

Lots shipped by Greystone on July 8 and July 14, 2025 continue to delaminate: 18% of sampled pouches failed seal integrity testing. Greystone's 30-day cure period expired on July 3, 2025. Our costs of rework, product write-offs and customer credits from the defective Greystone lots now total $286,900.

### Item 6 — Talbot termination letter, August 15, 2025

**From:** Celeste Marchetti, Chief Operating Officer
**To:** Harold Imbriano, President, Greystone Packaging Co.
**Date:** August 15, 2025

Greystone has failed to cure the material breach described in our June 3 notice within thirty days. Talbot hereby terminates the Packaging Supply Agreement under Section 9.2, effective immediately. Under Section 9.4, Talbot has no further minimum purchase obligations for Contract Year 3 or thereafter. Talbot reserves all rights, including to recover its damages from the nonconforming lots.
