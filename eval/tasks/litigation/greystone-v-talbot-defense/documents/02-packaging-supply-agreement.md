# PACKAGING SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Packaging Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of March 1, 2023 (the "Effective Date") between **Greystone Packaging Co.**, an Ohio corporation ("Supplier"), and **Talbot Fresh Foods, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Buyer").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Contract Year" means each twelve-month period beginning on March 1, the first Contract Year beginning on the Effective Date.

1.2 "Products" means the laminated flexible pouches and lidding films described in Schedule 1, manufactured to the Specifications.

## 3. TERM

3.1 This Agreement continues for three (3) Contract Years from the Effective Date unless terminated earlier under Section 9.

## 4. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

4.1 Buyer shall purchase Products having an aggregate invoice value of not less than $2,500,000 in each Contract Year (the "Minimum Annual Purchase").

4.2 Supplier shall invoice any shortfall within thirty (30) days after the end of the Contract Year, and Buyer shall pay the invoice within thirty (30) days.

4.3 If Buyer's purchases in a Contract Year are less than the Minimum Annual Purchase, Buyer's sole liability for the shortfall shall be a fee equal to thirty-five percent (35%) of the difference between the Minimum Annual Purchase and Buyer's actual purchases in that Contract Year (the "Shortfall Fee"). The parties agree that the Shortfall Fee represents Supplier's lost margin and is a reasonable pre-estimate of Supplier's damages.

## 7. QUALITY AND WARRANTY

7.1 Supplier warrants that all Products will conform to the Specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship, including seal and laminate integrity.

7.2 Buyer may reject any lot in which more than two percent (2%) of sampled units fail the Specifications.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach.

9.4 Upon termination by Buyer under Section 9.2, Buyer shall have no further minimum purchase obligations under Section 4 for the Contract Year in which termination occurs or any later Contract Year.

## 12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

12.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS OR LOSS OF BUSINESS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES. THIS SECTION 12.1 DOES NOT LIMIT THE SHORTFALL FEE UNDER SECTION 4.3.

## 14. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1 A party asserting a dispute arising out of this Agreement shall give the other party written notice of the dispute (a "Notice of Dispute").

14.2 For thirty (30) days after delivery of a Notice of Dispute, senior executives of the parties with authority to settle shall meet and negotiate in good faith to resolve the dispute.

14.3 If the dispute is not resolved within that thirty-day period, either party may submit the dispute to non-binding mediation administered by JAMS in Chicago, Illinois. No party may commence litigation concerning a dispute until the mediation has concluded or sixty (60) days have passed after the request for mediation, except to seek temporary injunctive relief.

## 15. GENERAL

15.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

15.2 **Notices.** Notices to Buyer shall be sent to: Talbot Fresh Foods, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, 1800 Rowell Avenue, Joliet, Illinois 60433.

15.3 **Contractual Limitations Period.** No action arising out of this Agreement may be brought by either party more than one (1) year after the cause of action has accrued.
