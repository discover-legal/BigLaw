# MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

**Lumen Ridge Software, Inc. — Form MSA v4.2 — as sent to Corvane Logistics Group, Inc. on September 2, 2026**

This Master Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date between **Lumen Ridge Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and **Corvane Logistics Group, Inc.**, a Texas corporation ("Customer").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Effective Date" means the date of the last signature below.

1.2 "Fees" means the subscription fees set out in each Order Form.

1.3 "Services" means Provider's RouteLens fleet-optimization platform and related support described in an Order Form.

## 2. SERVICES

2.1 Provider shall make the Services available to Customer during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement and the Service Level Agreement in Exhibit B (the "SLA").

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Customer shall pay all Fees within thirty (30) days after the invoice date.

4.2 Overdue amounts bear interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by law, if lower.

4.3 Fees are fixed for the initial Subscription Term. Thereafter Provider may increase Fees on renewal by giving at least sixty (60) days' notice.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 The initial Subscription Term is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date and renews for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

6.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice.

6.3 Upon termination for any reason other than Provider's uncured material breach, Customer shall pay all Fees for the remainder of the then-current Subscription Term.

## 8. DATA SECURITY

8.1 Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards consistent with ISO/IEC 27001 for Customer Data.

8.2 **Audit.** Upon at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice, and not more than once in any twelve (12) month period, Customer may audit Provider's compliance with this Section 8 by means of Provider's then-current SOC 2 Type II report and a written security questionnaire.

## 9. WARRANTIES

9.1 Provider warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the Documentation for a period of ninety (90) days after each release is made available to Customer (the "Warranty Period").

9.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS SECTION 9, PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 11 OR CUSTOMER'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

10.2 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Services infringe a United States patent, copyright or trademark, and shall pay any damages finally awarded.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 Provider shall maintain cyber liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per claim.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that either party may assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, provided the assignee is not a competitor of the non-assigning party.

13.2 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

13.3 **Non-Solicitation.** During the Subscription Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was involved in the Services.

## EXHIBIT B — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

B.1 Provider shall make the Services available 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, excluding Scheduled Maintenance.

B.2 If availability falls below 99.9%, Customer shall receive a service credit of 5% of the monthly Fees for each full 0.5% shortfall, provided that service credits in any month shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the monthly Fees for that month.

B.3 Service credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures.
