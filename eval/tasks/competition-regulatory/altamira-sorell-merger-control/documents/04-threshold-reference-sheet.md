# MERGER-CONTROL THRESHOLD REFERENCE SHEET (INTERNAL)

**Antitrust practice group — reference figures for transactions closing in 2025.** Figures are reproduced for convenience; always confirm against the current Federal Register notice and the text of the EU Merger Regulation before advising.

## A. United States — Hart-Scott-Rodino Act (thresholds effective February 21, 2025)

A.1 **Size-of-transaction.** A transaction is potentially reportable if, as a result, the acquiring person would hold voting securities and/or assets of the acquired person valued in excess of $126.4 million. Voting securities of the same issuer already held by the acquiring person are aggregated with those being acquired (16 C.F.R. 801.13).

A.2 **Size-of-person.** For transactions valued above $126.4 million but not more than $505.8 million, the size-of-person test must also be met: generally, one person must have annual net sales or total assets of $252.9 million or more and the other person must have annual net sales or total assets of $25.3 million or more. Transactions valued above $505.8 million are reportable regardless of the size of the parties.

A.3 **Foreign issuer exemption (16 C.F.R. 802.51).** An acquisition by a U.S. person of voting securities of a foreign issuer is exempt unless the issuer (including all entities it controls) holds assets located in the United States having an aggregate total value of over $126.4 million, or made aggregate sales in or into the United States of over $126.4 million in its most recent fiscal year.

A.4 **Filing fees (2025).**

| Value of transaction | Fee |
|---|---|
| Less than $179.4 million | $30,000 |
| $179.4 million or more but less than $555.5 million | $105,000 |
| $555.5 million or more but less than $1.111 billion | $265,000 |
| $1.111 billion or more but less than $2.222 billion | $425,000 |
| $2.222 billion or more but less than $5.555 billion | $850,000 |
| $5.555 billion or more | $2,390,000 |

A.5 **Waiting period.** The initial waiting period is 30 days from filing (15 days for cash tender offers and bankruptcy sales). If the period would end on a weekend or federal holiday, it ends on the next business day. Parties may not close, and the acquirer may not obtain beneficial ownership or operational control of the target, before the waiting period expires or is terminated ("gun-jumping"; civil penalties accrue per day of violation).

## B. European Union — Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 (EUMR)

B.1 **Article 1(2).** A concentration has an EU dimension where (a) the combined aggregate worldwide turnover of all the undertakings concerned is more than EUR 5,000 million; and (b) the aggregate EU-wide turnover of each of at least two of the undertakings concerned is more than EUR 250 million; unless each of the undertakings concerned achieves more than two-thirds of its aggregate EU-wide turnover within one and the same Member State.

B.2 **Article 1(3).** A concentration that does not meet Article 1(2) has an EU dimension where (a) the combined aggregate worldwide turnover of all the undertakings concerned is more than EUR 2,500 million; (b) in each of at least three Member States, the combined aggregate turnover of all the undertakings concerned is more than EUR 100 million; (c) in each of at least three Member States included for the purpose of point (b), the aggregate turnover of each of at least two of the undertakings concerned is more than EUR 25 million; and (d) the aggregate EU-wide turnover of each of at least two of the undertakings concerned is more than EUR 100 million; unless each of the undertakings concerned achieves more than two-thirds of its aggregate EU-wide turnover within one and the same Member State.

B.3 **Consequences.** A concentration with an EU dimension must be notified to the European Commission prior to implementation and may not be implemented before it has been declared compatible with the internal market (Article 7(1) standstill). Phase I review lasts 25 working days from notification (extendable to 35). National merger control of EU Member States does not apply to a concentration with an EU dimension (one-stop shop, Article 21(3)).
