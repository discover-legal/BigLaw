# PROJECT LANTERN — TRANSACTION SUMMARY FOR MERGER-CONTROL COUNSEL

**Prepared by:** Altamira Health Technologies, Inc. — Corporate Development
**Date:** September 15, 2025
**Status:** Board approval obtained September 11, 2025; signing targeted for October 1, 2025

## 1. Parties

**Acquirer.** Altamira Health Technologies, Inc. ("Altamira"), a Delaware corporation headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Altamira is the ultimate parent entity of the Altamira group; no natural person or entity controls it. Altamira designs and manufactures in-vitro diagnostic instruments and consumables.

**Target.** Sorell Diagnostics B.V. ("Sorell"), a private limited company organized under the laws of the Netherlands with its registered office in Eindhoven. Sorell is the ultimate parent of its group, which includes Sorell Diagnostics USA, Inc. (a wholly owned distribution subsidiary in Raleigh, North Carolina). Sorell manufactures point-of-care coagulation analyzers and test cartridges at its Eindhoven plant.

## 2. Structure

2.1 Altamira will acquire 92% of the issued voting shares of Sorell from the Van Duren family holding company and Sorell's management shareholders, for a cash purchase price of $212,500,000 (the "Purchase Price").

2.2 Altamira already holds 8% of Sorell's voting shares, acquired in a 2022 minority investment. Corporate Development values that existing 8% stake at $16,000,000 as of today. Following closing Altamira will hold 100% of Sorell.

2.3 There is no earn-out or deferred consideration. Sorell's net debt at closing is expected to be approximately $18,000,000 and will be repaid by Sorell from cash on hand at closing; it does not form part of the Purchase Price.

## 3. Rationale and overlap

3.1 Both parties sell point-of-care coagulation analyzers to hospitals and anticoagulation clinics. Altamira's analyzer is sold mainly in the United States and Germany; Sorell's is sold mainly in the Netherlands, Germany and France. The deal team expects the combined business to be the leading supplier of point-of-care coagulation analyzers in the EEA.

3.2 Sorell's cartridge line has no overlap with Altamira's products.

## 4. Timetable

4.1 Signing: October 1, 2025. Altamira intends to submit any required United States premerger notification on October 6, 2025.

4.2 Closing is targeted for the earliest date permitted after all mandatory merger-control clearances are obtained.

## 5. Integration planning (from the Corporate Development integration workstream)

5.1 Integration planning will be conducted through a clean team for competitively sensitive information.

5.2 To capture revenue synergies quickly, Altamira's commercial team will assume responsibility for Sorell's US pricing decisions from signing, and Sorell's US sales representatives will begin reporting to Altamira's Vice President of Sales on October 1, 2025.

## 6. Questions for counsel

(b) Does the transaction have an EU dimension under the EU Merger Regulation, and if so what are the consequences for timing?

(c) Any substantive or process concerns we should address before signing?
