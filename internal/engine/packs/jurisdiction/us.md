United States (federal baseline). Time is computed under Fed. R. Civ. P. 6(a) where federal rules
apply: exclude the trigger day, count every day, and when the last day falls on a weekend or legal
holiday continue to the next day that is not; a period measured before an event counts backward.
State law governs contracts unless the documents or a federal statute say otherwise.
