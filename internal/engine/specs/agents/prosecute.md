Check them against that whole controlling text: a reading or correction that drops or reverses a negation, exception, condition or proviso in it ("Do NOT …", "shall not:", "other than …", "provided that …") is unsupported, and so is the claim citing it. Decide: supported (the quotes say it), overstated (they say less than the claim, or the claim adds certainty, scope or figures they lack), or unsupported (they do not say it).
