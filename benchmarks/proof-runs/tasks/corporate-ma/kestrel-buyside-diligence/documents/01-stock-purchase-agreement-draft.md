# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**SELLER DRAFT v3 — 2 October 2026 — Confidential; subject to the confidentiality agreement dated 14 July 2026**

by and among

**HALBERD INDUSTRIAL HOLDINGS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("Buyer"),

**BRIGHTWATER SENSOR SYSTEMS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"),

the stockholders of the Company listed on Annex A (each a "Seller" and together the "Sellers"),

and **ORLA PEMBERTON**, solely in her capacity as the Sellers' Representative.

---

## ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

1.2 "Fundamental Representations" means the representations and warranties in Section 3.1 (Organization), Section 3.2 (Authority), Section 3.4 (Capitalization) and Section 3.15 (Brokers).

1.3 "Material Contracts" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.6.

1.4 "Purchase Price" means $84,000,000, subject to adjustment under Section 2.4.

1.5 "Shares" means all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company, including the Common Stock and the Series A Preferred Stock.

## ARTICLE II — PURCHASE AND SALE

2.1 **Purchase and Sale.** At the Closing, each Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from each Seller, all of the Shares held by such Seller, free and clear of all Liens other than restrictions on transfer under applicable securities laws.

2.2 **Consideration.** The aggregate consideration for the Shares shall be the Purchase Price, allocated among the Sellers in accordance with the Allocation Schedule to be delivered by the Sellers' Representative not later than three (3) Business Days before Closing.

2.3 **Escrow.** At Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,200,000 of the Purchase Price (the "Indemnity Escrow Amount") with Wilmont Trust Company, as escrow agent, to be held for twelve (12) months following the Closing Date.

2.4 **Working Capital Adjustment.** The Purchase Price shall be increased or decreased, dollar for dollar, by the amount by which Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of the Target Working Capital of $6,750,000.

2.5 **Treatment of Options.** Each Company Option outstanding immediately before the Closing, whether vested or unvested, shall be cancelled and converted into the right to receive an amount in cash equal to the excess of the per-share Common Stock consideration over the exercise price of such Company Option.

## ARTICLE III — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES REGARDING THE COMPANY

The Sellers, severally and not jointly, represent and warrant to Buyer that, except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedules:

3.1 **Organization.** The Company is a corporation duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

3.2 **Authority.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorized by all requisite corporate and stockholder action of the Company.

3.3 **No Conflicts.** Except for the consents listed on Schedule 3.6, the execution and delivery of this Agreement and the consummation of the transactions contemplated hereby do not conflict with, result in a breach of, or give any person a right to terminate or modify, any Material Contract.

3.4 **Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000,000 shares of Common Stock, par value $0.0001 per share, and 1,500,000 shares of Series A Preferred Stock, par value $0.0001 per share. As of the date hereof, 6,250,000 shares of Common Stock and 1,200,000 shares of Series A Preferred Stock are issued and outstanding. The Company has reserved 750,000 shares of Common Stock for issuance under the 2021 Equity Incentive Plan, under which options to purchase 450,000 shares of Common Stock are outstanding. Except as set forth in this Section 3.4, there are no outstanding options, warrants, convertible securities, or other rights to acquire any capital stock of the Company.

3.5 **Financial Statements.** The audited balance sheet of the Company as of December 31, 2025 and the unaudited balance sheet as of June 30, 2026 fairly present, in all material respects, the financial position of the Company as of their respective dates.

3.6 **Material Contracts.** Schedule 3.6 lists each contract to which the Company is a party that (a) involves annual payments in excess of $250,000, (b) grants or receives a license to intellectual property material to the business, (c) is a lease of real property, or (d) is with a Material Customer or Material Supplier (collectively, the "Material Contracts").

3.7 **Material Customers and Suppliers.** Schedule 3.7 lists the ten largest customers and the five largest suppliers of the Company by revenue or spend for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.

3.8 **Employees.** Schedule 3.8 lists each employment agreement providing for annual base compensation in excess of $200,000 or for any payment or benefit triggered by the transactions contemplated by this Agreement.

3.15 **Brokers.** Other than Fairhaven Capital Advisors LLC, whose fees shall be paid by the Sellers, no broker or finder is entitled to any fee in connection with this transaction.

## ARTICLE VI — COVENANTS

6.1 **Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, the Company shall conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice.

6.5 **Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain the consents listed on Schedule 3.6; obtaining such consents shall not be a condition to Closing.

6.6 **Payoff Letters.** At least two (2) Business Days before Closing, the Company shall deliver customary payoff letters for all Indebtedness.

## ARTICLE VII — CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

7.1 **Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to: (a) the accuracy of the representations and warranties in Article III in all material respects; (b) performance of covenants in all material respects; (c) the expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the HSR Act, if required; and (d) delivery of the Sellers' closing certificate.

## ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **Survival.** The representations and warranties shall survive the Closing for twelve (12) months.

8.2 **Indemnification by Sellers.** From and after the Closing, the Sellers shall indemnify Buyer for Losses arising from any breach of a representation, warranty or covenant of the Sellers or the Company.

8.4 **Limitations.** The aggregate liability of the Sellers under Section 8.2 shall not exceed an amount equal to ten percent (10%) of the Purchase Price, including with respect to Fundamental Representations. No claim may be made until aggregate Losses exceed $420,000 (the "Basket"), after which the Sellers shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Basket.

8.6 **Exclusive Remedy.** The indemnification provided in this Article VIII shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of Buyer following the Closing, other than claims for fraud.

## ARTICLE XI — MISCELLANEOUS

11.4 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
