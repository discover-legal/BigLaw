# COMPONENT SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Component Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of May 15, 2023 (the "Effective Date") between **Norrland Microdevices AB**, a company organized under the laws of Sweden ("Supplier"), and **Brightwater Sensor Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Buyer").

## 1. SUPPLY

1.1 **Products.** Supplier shall manufacture and sell to Buyer the MEMS pressure-sensing die described in Exhibit A (the "Products") in accordance with Buyer's purchase orders issued under this Agreement.

1.2 **Exclusivity.** During the Term, Buyer shall purchase from Supplier not less than one hundred percent (100%) of Buyer's requirements for MEMS pressure-sensing die used in the Brightwater HydroSense product line. Supplier is Buyer's sole qualified source for the Products.

## 2. MINIMUM PURCHASE COMMITMENT

2.1 Buyer shall purchase Products having an aggregate invoice value of not less than $2,400,000 in each Contract Year (the "Minimum Commitment").

2.2 If Buyer's purchases in any Contract Year are less than the Minimum Commitment, Buyer shall pay Supplier, within thirty (30) days after the end of such Contract Year, an amount equal to the shortfall (the "Shortfall Payment"). Shortfall Payments are liquidated damages and not a penalty.

## 3. PRICE AND PAYMENT

3.1 Prices are set out in Exhibit B and are fixed through the end of the second Contract Year, after which Supplier may adjust prices once per Contract Year by not more than the percentage change in the Swedish producer price index.

3.2 Buyer shall pay each invoice within forty-five (45) days after the invoice date.

## 4. TERM

4.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of five (5) years, and shall renew automatically for successive two (2) year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least twelve (12) months before the end of the then-current term.

## 12. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

12.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Supplier may assign its right to receive payment to a financing institution.

12.2 Buyer shall notify Supplier in writing within ten (10) Business Days after any Change of Control of Buyer. A Change of Control of Buyer shall be deemed an assignment of this Agreement. Upon a Change of Control of Buyer, Supplier may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice given within ninety (90) days after receiving notice of the Change of Control.

12.3 "Change of Control" means (a) the acquisition by any person or group of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of a party, or (b) a merger or consolidation of a party in which the holders of its voting securities immediately before the transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of the surviving entity.

## 14. GENERAL

14.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Sweden, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

14.2 **Disputes.** Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the SCC Arbitration Institute in Stockholm.
