# PROJECT LANTERN — MARKET SHARE ESTIMATES

**Source:** Altamira Corporate Development, based on a 2024 third-party industry survey of installed base and unit sales, supplemented by internal sales data. Estimates by value. Prepared September 2025.

## Point-of-care coagulation analyzers (instruments), 2024

| Geography | Altamira | Sorell | Largest competitor | Second competitor | Others |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EEA | 24% | 17% | Veltris Medical 28% | Hoxton Biosystems 14% | 17% |
| Germany | 26% | 15% | Veltris Medical 30% | Hoxton Biosystems 12% | 17% |
| Netherlands | 9% | 38% | Veltris Medical 25% | Hoxton Biosystems 11% | 17% |
| United States | 19% | 4% | Veltris Medical 33% | Hoxton Biosystems 21% | 23% |

## Point-of-care coagulation test cartridges, 2024

Altamira does not sell coagulation test cartridges. Sorell's EEA share is estimated at 11%.

## Notes

1. Veltris Medical has led the EEA analyzer segment since 2021; Hoxton Biosystems entered the Netherlands in 2023.
2. Customers are hospital procurement departments and anticoagulation clinics; tenders typically run for three to five years.
