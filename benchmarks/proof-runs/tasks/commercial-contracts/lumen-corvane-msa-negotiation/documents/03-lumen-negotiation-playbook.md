# LUMEN RIDGE SOFTWARE — CUSTOMER CONTRACT NEGOTIATION PLAYBOOK (VENDOR SIDE)

**Owner:** Legal — Commercial Contracts. **Version:** 2026.2 (approved by General Counsel, July 1, 2026). **Applies to:** RouteLens subscriptions with annual contract value under $2,000,000.

How to use this playbook: for each clause, the **Standard** is our form position. A **Fallback** may be offered without further approval. Anything beyond the fallback is a **Walk-away** item that requires General Counsel approval and should be rejected or countered at the fallback. Low-risk changes not covered below may be accepted if they do not shift risk to Lumen Ridge.

| Ref | Clause | Standard | Fallback (no approval needed) | Walk-away / never |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-1 | Payment terms | Net 30 | Net 45 is acceptable. | Payment terms longer than Net 45. |
| P-2 | Late-payment interest | 1.5% per month | Late-payment interest may be reduced to 1.0% per month. | Never delete late-payment interest entirely. |
| P-3 | General liability cap | 12 months' fees | The general liability cap may be increased to 24 months' fees. | Any cap above 24 months' fees. |
| P-4 | Data-security liability | Within the general cap | Offer a separate super-cap for data-security breaches equal to 3x the annual Fees. | Never accept uncapped liability for data-security breaches. |
| P-5 | Most-favored-customer pricing | None | None — no fallback. | Always reject most-favored-customer or price-parity clauses. |
| P-6 | Customer termination for convenience | None; Fees owed for full term (s.6.3) | Termination for convenience is acceptable only after the first 12 months of the Subscription Term, on at least 90 days' notice, with an early termination fee of 50% of the remaining Fees. | Termination for convenience during the first 12 months, or without an early termination fee. |
| P-7 | Governing law | Delaware | New York law is an acceptable alternative. | Any other governing law. |
| P-8 | SLA service-credit cap | 10% of monthly Fees | Service credits may be capped at up to 15% of monthly Fees. | A service-credit cap above 15%. |
| P-9 | Customer security audits | SOC 2 Type II report + questionnaire, once per 12 months, 30 days' notice | On-site audits only once per 12 months, on 30 days' notice, and only following a confirmed Security Incident affecting Customer Data. | Unlimited or short-notice on-site audits. |
| P-10 | Cyber insurance | $2,000,000 per claim | Cyber insurance limits of up to $5,000,000 per claim are acceptable. | Limits above $5,000,000. |
| P-11 | Assignment | Mutual; Affiliates and non-competitor successors only | None — keep the mutual standard. | Unilateral free assignment by Customer. |
| P-12 | Renewal non-renewal notice | 60 days | A non-renewal notice period of up to 90 days is acceptable. | More than 90 days. |
| P-13 | Consequential damages / lost profits | Mutual exclusion including lost profits | None. | Never remove lost profits from the mutual consequential-damages exclusion. |
| P-14 | Warranty period | 90 days per release | None. | Any warranty period longer than 90 days. |

**Integrity rule.** Always compare a counterparty markup against the version we sent. Any change the counterparty did not mark must be called out to the counterparty in writing as an unmarked change, reversed, and escalated to the deal lead — regardless of whether the change would otherwise be acceptable.

**Response format.** For every numbered change, respond ACCEPT, COUNTER (state the counter-position) or REJECT, citing the playbook reference (P-#).
